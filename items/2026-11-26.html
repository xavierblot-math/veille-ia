Terence Tao (blog, guest post) — G.R.H. Polymath

  • Guest post by Anurag Sahay, Will Sawin and Lior Silberman proposing a Polymath-style project around Levent Alpöge's Lean certificate for the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, announced last Friday.
  • Goals: pool the worldwide validation efforts, extract a human-readable proof of RH/GRH, simplify the Lean certificate, clarify related literature, and generalize the new ideas buried in the machine proof.
  • The proof seems to touch many fields beyond number theory, so the work is expected to split into sub-projects; any resulting papers would follow the Polymath pseudonym tradition.
  • Discussion happens on a public Zulip (rh-polymath.zulipchat.com), readable without login.
  • 👉 A concrete entry point if you want to follow or join the effort to turn the formal proof into mathematics people understand.

Proofs and Prompts (Dan Romik) — On the AHM’s awkward position on the Riemann Hypothesis being solved

  • Romik (UC Davis, founder of Rhombic Research) goes through the Association for Human Mathematics' 20 November statement on the GRH announcement line by line and rebuts it.
  • He argues validity has always been mathematics' central value, not a new "corporate" one; understanding is a later goal the formal proof helps rather than hinders.
  • Against the certainty objection, he cites the Lean FRO's confidence in the proof and recalls that Wiles' 1993 argument had a gap, yet no one blamed him for announcing it.
  • He concedes one fair point (dependence on AI companies for research tools) but finds it irrelevant to whether RH is proved; ends by quoting Jared Lichtman's "AHMish mathematics" jibe.
  • 👉 Useful snapshot of the community split; Romik also wrote a post on verifying the Lean proof.
