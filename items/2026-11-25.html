Proofs and Prompts (Anonymous undergraduate) — I want MSE, not MO.

  • A short opinion piece arguing that mathematics should not be treated as an identity or something with outsized emotional weight; if it is, it should not be a profession.
  • Uses Math StackExchange vs MathOverflow as a metaphor: MO is rigorous and research-level (likened to current AI), MSE is more human, social and welcoming.
  • The author sides with MSE: the community should leave room for homework, curiosity and friendship, not only research questions — and points to MSE's larger participation as evidence of what people want.

Proofs and Prompts (Kei Beauduin) — Homage to Gold Miners

  • A rhyming allegorical poem (drafted with an LLM, idea and edits by the author, a Master's student at Kyushu) about mathematicians as miners whose trusted "wand" (proof verification) certifies real gold.
  • Drilling companies (AI) start weak, grow powerful, then dump huge loads of unsorted output in the miners' yard: there is gold inside, but extracting it costs time, effort and some dignity.
  • A pointed image of the refereeing/verification burden that mass AI-generated mathematics shifts onto humans; inspired by a remark of David Fisher.
